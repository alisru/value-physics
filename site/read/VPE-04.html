Executive Simulation Summary

This computational experiment evaluates a 600-citizen meso-scale micro-economy over a 3-year horizon (156 weekly timesteps). The system is subjected to three compounding exogenous crises: a 400 bps Central Bank rate hike (weeks 26–156), a 150% global fossil fuel crisis ($4.50/L diesel, weeks 52–156), and a 30-week severe agricultural drought (weeks 78–108).

  • Extractive Neoclassical Regime: Sinks into insolvency as debt expands from $6.0M to $41.46M AUD under compounding interest (9.5% commercial APR). 62 homes/farms are foreclosed, care burnout explodes to 29,799 units, and bread prices hit $6.20 AUD ($\Phi = 0.050, \Delta R = \$30.15\text{M}$).
  • Cohesive Village Protocol (Value Physics): Retains $4.32M AUD in its Sovereign Infrastructure Fund, amortizes debt to $5.40M AUD (0% real usury via Theorem 5), stores 4,689 tonnes of grain, 50,000 kL of water, and 100,000 kWh of microgrid energy, with 0 foreclosures, 0 burnout, and $1.15 AUD internal bread ($\Phi = 1.000, \Delta R = \$0.00$).

3. Structural Mechanisms Driving Simulation Outcomes

3.1 The Compounding Interest Singularity ($L(t) = L_0 e^{rt}$)

In the neoclassical regime, the village debt expanded from $6.0M to $41.46M AUD purely through interest rate compounding ($9.5\%$ APR on rolling overdrafts and mortgages). Under **Statutory Debt Bifurcation (Theorem 5)**, the Cohesive Village capped debt at invariant statutory face value ($C_{\text{base}}$), paying off $600,000 of principal linearly without a single cent of speculative usury ($E_{\text{premium}} \to 0$).

3.2 Triple-Buffer Shock Absorption

During the 30-week severe drought and fossil fuel crisis:

  • Water Buffer: The 50,000 kL gravity-fed catchment reservoir maintained 300L/person/day for all 600 citizens and irrigated organic greenhouses without drawing on external emergency water cartage.
  • Energy Buffer: The 100,000 kWh microgrid insulated the bakery, flour mill, and cold storage from external diesel price spikes ($4.50/L), keeping internal milling exergy costs strictly at $0.08/kWh.
  • Caloric Buffer ($TS_0$): The 4,689t grain silo supplied continuous flour to the village bakery while supporting external export sales ($520 AUD/t) that financed all necessary external medical and hardware imports.
