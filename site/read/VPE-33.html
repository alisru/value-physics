1. Executive Blueprint for Local Government Areas (LGAs)

1.1 Legal Authorities & Regulatory Grounding

Regional municipal councils in New South Wales possess explicit statutory powers to establish and participate in mutual corporations, public trusts, and shared regional service entities:

  • Section 358 of the Local Government Act 1993 (NSW): Authorizes municipal councils to form or participate in corporations, trusts, and joint ventures with Minister for Local Government approval to deliver essential community infrastructure.
  • Part 5C & Section 66 of the Corporations Act 2001 (Cth): Governs Mutual Entities and Member-Owned Credit Societies, protecting non-distributive surplus allocation.
  • Sections 11 & 12 of the Regional Investment Corporation Act 2018 (Cth): Authorizes concessional agricultural loan administration and farm debt restructuring in regional drought zones.
