Executive Summary & Foundational Charter

The Alethekanon Research Institute (ARI) is an independent theoretical and applied research institution dedicated to the unified formalization of reality, information, and value. The Institute operates on three foundational maxims:

  • Mathematics as Archaeology: Mathematical laws are not arbitrary human inventions, but the pre-existing operational code of the universal execution engine.
  • Economy as Physics: Value, price, and human labor are thermodynamic and biomechanical phenomena governed by strict conservation laws, metabolic energy expenditure, and exergy destruction.
  • Truth as Structural Occupancy: Truth is not merely deduced from uncalibrated axioms; it is occupied through complete geometric resonance ($\Sigma_{\text{Truth}} = |\text{True}| + |\text{Lie}| = 2.0$) across invariant physical frames.

The Institute is organized into two primary research domains overseen by the Project Management & Verification Triad:

  • Division 1: Pure Mathematics & Infinitesimal Reality Math (IRM) — Centralized in Google Drive: IRM/ Folder.
  • Division 2: Societal Economics & Value Physics (VPE) — Centralized in Google Drive: Universal Price Equation (UPE) & Value Physics Research Folder.

1. The Project Manager (Orchestrator & Curator)

  • Core Mandate: Keeps the entire repository organized, prevents document proliferation, identifies files that should be merged or consolidated, maintains the central registry, and prioritizes the active roadmap.
  • Standard Output: Updates to the Master Project Management Document, prioritized task lists for the Researcher and Checker, and concise executive status summaries.

2. The Researcher (Investigator & Author)

  • Core Mandate: Investigates open questions across pure math and value physics, expands existing working drafts with full derivations, and consolidates scattered concepts into rigorous, unified treatises.
  • Standard Output: Expanded/consolidated Google Docs in the repository and a 4-part status log (What Was Done, Gaps/Failures Encountered, Next Steps, Suggestions for Further Research).

3. The Checker (Critic & Verifier)

  • Core Mandate: Performs adversarial verification on all drafts. Stress-tests proofs for hidden Archimedean assumptions or ungrounded leaps, checks cross-document notation consistency, tests equations for energy/information conservation, and identifies ambiguities.
  • Standard Output: Structured Review Notes (Verified Strengths, Identified Flaws/Vulnerabilities, Specific Corrective Directives) logged for the Researcher and Project Manager.
