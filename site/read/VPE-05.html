Executive Conceptual Overview

Single-scalar economic theory compresses all exchange into a one-dimensional nominal dollar figure (P), blinding markets to the direction, perspective, and moral geometry of economic energy. In Value Physics, value is a multi-perspective vector operating across four cardinal orientations:

  • WEST (Work-Effort-Skill/Time Token): The Producer / Upstream Input Perspective. Measures sunk physical difficulty, thermodynamic exergy (P_e), biomechanical ATP (P_b), and training density (s).
  • EAST (Effect-Actual-Service/Time Token): The Consumer / Downstream Receiver Perspective. Measures realized biological sustenance, urgency relief (U → 0), and reciprocal life-enablement.
  • BEST (Beneficent-Ecological-Social/Time Token): The Systemic / Regenerative Surplus Perspective (+Z). Measures substrate well-deepening (R_n ↑), open protocols (A+), and long-term carrying capacity expansion (I_a - D_a).
  • WORST (Waste-Overload-Rent/Scarcity-Toll Token): The Parasitic / Extractive Toll Perspective (-Z). Measures artificial enclosure (A-), rentier extraction tolls (R_a → 1.0), and the Reality Gap Integral (Δ R).

2. Detailed Mathematical Deconstruction of the 4 Tokens

2.1 WEST: The Invariant Sunk Difficulty Floor

WEST tracks what was irreversibly spent by biological humans and the earth. It is strictly conservative: you cannot forge difficulty. An hour of heavy trade labor with danger and cognitive load (s=4.5, e=3.4 METs, d=0.25, c=0.40) generates 1.62 WEST Tokens, anchoring the thermodynamic invariant base price P_H.

2.2 EAST: The Reciprocal Effect Token

EAST measures the realized human outcome of the service or product delivered. While WEST answers "How hard was it to make?", EAST answers "What did it actually do for the receiver's persistence?":

  • Urgency Relief (Δ U): How much did the provision reduce the receiver's existential urgency (U(t) → 0)? Providing insulin to a diabetic or water in the desert delivers maximum EAST relief.
  • Reciprocal Alignment (WEST ↔ EAST): In a fair transaction, 1.0 WEST of difficulty delivered yields 1.0 EAST of recognized utility, establishing bilateral moral closure without residue.

2.3 BEST: The Generative Systemic Surplus Token

BEST quantifies work that expands the carry-capacity (R_n) of the entire settlement without being consumed in bilateral exchange:

  • Planting windbreaks and regenerating topsoil nitrogen (dR_n/dt > 0).
  • Installing community solar microgrids and open-source tooling (A+).
  • Supporting dependents and cultural festivals (Plane 4 Lyrical Refreshment).
  • BEST tokens represent the physical surplus Surplus = I_a - D_a > 0 that funds research, tool development, and inter-generational resilience.

2.4 WORST: The Unearned Extraction & Toll Metric

WORST is the anti-token—the exact mathematical scalar tracking economic parasitism:

  • Rentier Tolls (R_a): Supermarket shelf listing fees, pharmaceutical patent gouging, and monopoly access fees.
  • Coercive Surcharges ((γ - 1) P_H): The price markup extracted purely because the buyer is under survival desperation.
  • Reality Gap Debt (Δ R): The stored unhedged deficit when nominal prices are held artificially below physical reproduction costs (P_nominal < P_e + P_b).
  • The Law of WORST Dissipation: In a cohesive economy, the objective is to drive WORST → 0, forcing all financial claims to be backed by verifiable WEST or BEST tokens.

P_x = α · N_x + β · W_x where α >> β
