Executive Summary & Research Abstract

While single-community economic models demonstrate local self-sufficiency, real-world regional resilience depends on inter-regional trade, resource diversity, and spatial shock absorption. Agricultural yields, water catchments, and solar irradiance vary dramatically across geographic terrain, leaving isolated communities vulnerable to localized droughts, supply chain failures, and macro-financial shocks.

This research treatise expands the Cohesive Village framework into a 10-Node Regional Network Simulation across regional New South Wales (6,000 citizens, 2,000 households, 100 broadacre farming enterprises, managing 40,000 hectares across Regional NSW, Gilgandra, Gunnedah, Narrabri, Dubbo, Mudgee, Tamworth, Warren, Walgett, and Moree). The network is simulated across 156 dynamic weeks under compound asymmetric stressors:

  • Asymmetric Climate Drought: A 70-week severe drought causing an 85% crop yield collapse in northern cropping nodes (Walgett, Moree, Narrabri) while central and southern nodes maintain moderate production.
  • Monetary & Energetic Shocks: A simultaneous Reserve Bank of Australia (RBA) cash rate hike from 0.10% to 4.35% (+425 bps) and a diesel fuel price spike from $1.45 to $2.25/L.
  • Catastrophic Finite Boundary Stress Testing (OPEN-06): A 1,000-run Monte Carlo evaluation under multi-year regional catchment depletion and 100% prolonged rainfall blackout to determine exact carrying capacity exhaustion boundaries ($T_{\text{exhaust}}$) and municipal survival probability distributions.

We compare the Neoclassical Status Quo (bilateral fiat spot markets, commercial bank compound debt, and retail supermarket duopoly gatekeeping) against the Value Physics Tri-Fold Network (decentralized Book 1 mutual credit, strategic Book 2 physical buffers, Theorem 5 linear amortization, and the regularized Inter-Sovereign Trade Tensor $\mathbf{\Xi}_{\text{trade}}$).

The simulation proves that under the Value Physics protocol:

  • Total Regional Debt is linearly reduced from $180.0M to $148.92M AUD ($31.08M AUD principal amortized at $0.0%$ interest), compared to debt ballooning to $198.18M AUD under orthodox banking.
  • Regional Retail Bread Prices remain strictly invariant at $1.15 AUD ($P_H$), whereas supermarket prices escalate to $5.65 AUD (peak $5.88 AUD) under neoclassical margin defense.
  • Inter-Sovereign Trade Tensor ($\mathbf{\Xi}_{\text{trade}}$) executed 340 decentralized bilateral commodity transfers (3,708.2 tonnes of grain counter-settled in solar MWh and clinical care hours), maintaining 100% regional food security and zero insolvencies.
  • Finite Boundary Resilience: Under catastrophic regional mega-droughts, Value Physics strategic physical buffers ($TS_0 \in [12, 52\text{ weeks}]$) extend community survival horizons by $2.5\times$ to $5.0\times$ compared to the commercial banking status quo, proving that solvency is physically bounded by Book 2 reserve depth and replenishment velocity.

2. Mathematical Formulation of the Dynamic Network

2.1 Dynamic Leontief-Differential Production System

Each node $i \in \{1, \dots, 10\}$ produces across the 5 sectors with time-varying technical coefficients $\mathbf{A}_i(t)$:

$$\frac{d\mathbf{x}_i(t)}{dt} = (\mathbf{I} - \mathbf{A}_i(t))\mathbf{x}_i(t) - \mathbf{c}_i(t) + \sum_{j=1}^{10} \mathbf{t}_{ji}(t) - \sum_{j=1}^{10} \mathbf{t}_{ij}(t)$$

Where:

  • $\mathbf{x}_i(t)$ is the gross output vector of node $i$.
  • $\mathbf{c}_i(t)$ is local non-discretionary consumption (food, power, water, housing, care for 600 citizens).
  • $\mathbf{t}_{ji}(t)$ is the physical import commodity vector from node $j$ to node $i$.
  • $\mathbf{t}_{ij}(t)$ is the physical export commodity vector from node $i$ to node $j$.

2.2 The Inter-Sovereign Trade Tensor ($\mathbf{\Xi}_{\text{trade}}$)

When node $i$ experiences a localized physical deficit ($\text{Buffer}_i(t) < \text{Safety Threshold}$), it initiates a bilateral transfer with the nearest surplus node $j$. Settlement is governed by the regularized exchange tensor:

$$\mathbf{\Xi}_{\text{trade}, ij} = \frac{\text{WEST}_{\text{exported}, ij}}{\text{WEST}_{\text{imported}, ji}} \cdot \left[ \frac{R_{n,i} + \Delta R_{n,i}(t)}{R_{n,j} + \Delta R_{n,j}(t)} \right]$$

  • Physical transfer occurs immediately along the shortest road network path ($D_{ij}$).
  • Counter-settlement occurs in complementary physical capacity: a grain-importing node (e.g., drought-stricken Walgett) counter-settles by exporting excess Solar MWh or providing specialized agricultural machinery repairs, completely bypassing external fiat foreign exchange markets.

2.3 Theorem 5 Linear Amortization & Climatic Hazard Pause

Productive capital debt across all 100 broadacre enterprises ($18.0M AUD per village) amortizes strictly linearly:

$$\text{Obligation}_i(t) = \frac{C_{\text{principal}, i}}{T_{\text{term}}} \quad (r_{\text{compound}} \equiv 0.0\%)$$

During declared climate emergencies ($\text{Yield Drop} \ge 50\%$), debt amortization is automatically paused without penalty, preventing cash-flow distress.

4.3 Key Theoretical Insights from Boundary Stress Testing

  • The Invariant Physical Limit: Under complete climate collapse, the survival horizon of an economy is mathematically bounded by the depth of its physical Book 2 reserves: $$T_{\text{exhaust}} \approx T_{\text{shock\_start}} + \frac{S_{\text{buffer}, 0}}{\text{Demand} - \text{Depleted\_Inflow}}$$ Financial liquidity, debt restructuring, or money printing cannot manufacture calories or water out of nothing.
  • Neoclassical Institutional Failure: Under orthodox commercial banking, farm enterprises fail at Week 44.0 due to financial liquidity starvation and debt compounding ($>\$402.5\text{M AUD}$ total debt), even while physical grain remains stored in commercial silos.
  • Value Physics Substrate Optimization: Value Physics decouples financial insolvency from physical survival. Under identical physical shocks, Book 2 strategic silos and 0% mutual credit clearing extend regional community survival to the exact physical limit of the stored exergy ($81.1\text{ weeks}$ for a 1-year buffer; $146.4\text{ weeks}$ for a 2-year buffer).

5. Key Macroeconomic & Network Findings

5.1 The Failure of Decentralized Spot Markets under Asymmetric Shocks

Under the Neoclassical baseline, when a multi-year drought strikes Walgett, Moree, and Narrabri:

  • Local farmgate grain revenue drops by 85%, while diesel costs and commercial bank overdraft rates (7.20% APR) escalate.
  • All $1.50M of Farm Management Deposits (FMDs) per village are drained within 12 months, forcing farmers into compounding debt ($59.15M across the three northern nodes).
  • Grain spot market prices spike, but farmers have no grain to sell. Supermarkets raise shelf prices to $5.65 AUD to protect margins, triggering regional food poverty.

5.2 Inter-Village Buffer Balancing via $\mathbf{\Xi}_{\text{trade}}$

Under the Value Physics Cohesive Grid:

  • Northern drought nodes draw from local strategic silos ($TS_0$ Buffer). When silos reach safety floors, the automated matching engine routes surplus grain from Gunnedah (65t/wk base) and Warren (55t/wk base) across 100–180 km transport corridors.
  • Across 156 weeks, 3,708.2 tonnes of wheat were dynamically shifted across the 10 nodes over 340 transactions.
  • Walgett counter-settled grain imports by dispatching 2,450 MWh of surplus solar electricity back into the regional 11kV interconnector, while Dubbo provided 4,200 hours of clinical tele-care.
  • Zero farmers defaulted, zero debt compounded, and the shelf price of bread remained invariant at $1.15 AUD.
