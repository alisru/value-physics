Abstract

Orthodox neoclassical economics treats economic commodities and consumer prices as static, observer-independent scalars resulting from intersecting supply and demand curves ($P = f(S, D)$), enforcing the "law of one price" where a physical good costs an identical dollar amount regardless of the transacting agent's economic position. This treatise formalizes the transition from classical scalar pricing to the Quantum Universal Price Equation (Q-UPE) and Relational Value Tensors, establishing that an economic good or service is an entangled bipartite density operator $\hat{\rho}_{\text{item}}(Y)$ defined over the tensor product of an upstream physical transformation state space and an observer's financial state space.

First, we define the Upstream Process Superposition Vector $|\text{Good}{\text{process}}\rangle = \sum{k=1}^N \sqrt{w_k} e^{i \phi_k} |\text{Stage}k\rangle$, decomposing a commodity across its Leontief supply chain into conserved thermodynamic exergy destruction ($P_e^{(k)}$), non-energy physical substrate replacement ($P_b^{(k)}$), metabolic human labor exertion ($U^{(k)}$, in WEST tokens), and institutional extractive rentier markups ($R_a^{(k)}$). Using the empirical benchmark of a 680g commercial sliced white loaf ($P{\text{retail}} = $4.10$, Fair RRP/D = $$3.03$), we formulate the Distortion Quotient ($\text{DQ}k = (P_k / P{\text{total}}) / (U_k / U_{\text{total}})$), proving that agricultural producers perform 56.7% of all real human labor while receiving only 14.6% of the final retail price ($\text{DQ} \approx 0.23\text{--}0.30$), whereas financialized supermarket shelf slotting and commercial REIT land rents capture 45.1% of the price while contributing only 3.4% of the human work ($\text{DQ} = 13.42$).|---|Second, we formulate the Observer-Relative State Collapse, proving that an item's observable difficulty eigenvalue is parameterized by the observer's weekly disposable income ($Y$), budget exhaustion velocity ($v_{\text{rel}}(Y) = C_{\text{subsistence}} / Y$), and Lorentz coercion factor $\gamma(v_{\text{rel}}(Y)) = (1 - v_{\text{rel}}^2)^{-1/2}$. Across the full Australian statutory welfare and wage ladder (from Youth Allowance at $$241.20/\text{wk}$ to Full-Time AWOTE at $$1,635.00/\text{wk}$ and high-net-worth benchmarks), we demonstrate that purchasing a single loaf of bread consumes 1.70% of total life and 38.76 minutes of labor for an unemployed youth ($\gamma = 22.37$, experienced metabolic burden = 48.09 Dilated-WEST), but collapses to 0.25% of income and 5.72 minutes for an average full-time worker ($\gamma = 1.02$), and just 11 seconds of labor for an ultra-high-net-worth earner ($\gamma = 1.00$).

Finally, we establish the Functional Affordance Decoherence Theorem: poverty accelerates $v_{\text{rel}} \to 1$, collapsing the quantum superposition of life options into a coercive survival struggle, while institutional rent extraction ($R_a$) acts as an open thermal bath driving environmental decoherence across civic supply chains.

1. Introduction & The Scalar Price Fallacy

Standard microeconomic doctrine asserts that a price tag ($P \in \mathbb{R}^+$) is an objective scalar conveying costless information about relative scarcity. In this paradigm, when a supermarket labels a loaf of bread at $$4.10$, this price represents an identical mathematical value across all buyers.

This formulation suffers from two fatal epistemological blind spots:

  • Upstream Incommensurability (The Erased Process): The scalar $$4.10$ conceals the complex multi-stage biophysical history of how the item was brought into being—conflating genuine thermodynamic exergy destruction and agricultural field labor with unearned monopolistic shelf-slotting fees and speculative commercial property rents.
  • Observer Invariance Fallacy: An exchange is fundamentally a physical measurement interaction between an agent and an item. For a citizen surviving below the physical subsistence threshold on Youth Allowance or the Disability Support Pension (DSP = $$618.85/\text{wk}$), paying $$4.10$ exerts a severe metabolic drag that threatens biological homeostasis. For a corporate executive earning $$6,500/\text{wk}$, the same transaction produces zero measurable disturbance on their balance sheet.

Value Physics resolves these pathologies by modeling economic items through the formalism of Relational Quantum Mechanics and Non-Archimedean State Coalgebras (established in Formal Paper 14).

2. The Upstream Process Superposition: Tracing the Complete Supply Chain

An economic commodity is a multi-stage Leontief process vector:

$$|\text{Item}{\text{process}}\rangle = \sum{k=1}^N \sqrt{w_k} e^{i \phi_k} |\text{Stage}_k\rangle$$

where each basis state $|\text{Stage}_k\rangle$ represents a discrete transformation stage carrying four conserved physical and institutional coordinates:

  • $P_e^{(k)}$: Thermodynamic energy exergy consumed (electricity, natural gas, diesel fuel).
  • $P_b^{(k)}$: Sunk physical substrate mass and machinery depreciation.
  • $U^{(k)}$: Direct metabolic human difficulty, quantified in WEST difficulty tokens (1 WEST $\approx$ 1 hour of normalized biological labor).
  • $R_a^{(k)}$: Institutional extractive drag fraction pocketed by financialized intermediaries.

3. The Relational Bipartite Density Matrix: Observer-Dependent Value Tensors

Under the Quantum Universal Price Equation, the total economic system is represented by the bipartite density matrix:

$$\hat{\rho}{\text{system}} = \hat{\rho}{\text{item}} \otimes \hat{\rho}_{\text{observer}}$$

An observer is characterized by their state vector $|\text{Observer}(Y, \tau, v_{\text{rel}})\rangle$:

  • $Y$: Weekly net disposable income.
  • $C_{\text{subsistence}}$: Weekly non-discretionary subsistence cost bundle ($$285.02/\text{wk}$ across the 14-item necessity basket).
  • $v_{\text{rel}} \equiv \min(0.999, C_{\text{subsistence}} / Y) \in [0, 1)$: Budget exhaustion velocity.
  • $\gamma(v_{\text{rel}}) \equiv (1 - v_{\text{rel}}^2)^{-1/2}$: Relativistic Lorentz survival coercion factor.

When the observer encounters the item, the observable measurement operator $\hat{\Omega}_{\text{INDEX}}$ yields the Income Normalised Difficulty Examination (INDEX) eigenvalue:

$$\langle \hat{\Omega}{\text{INDEX}} \rangle_Y = \left( \frac{P{\text{retail}}}{Y} \right) \times 100%$$

The stolen working life operator $\hat{\mathcal{T}}{\text{confiscate}}$ projects the unearned extractive premium $\Delta P = P{\text{retail}} - \text{RRP/D}$ into confiscated metabolic labor minutes:

$$\langle \hat{\mathcal{T}}{\text{confiscate}} \rangle_Y = \left( \frac{\Delta P}{w{\text{net}}(Y)} \right) \times 60 \text{ minutes}$$

4. The Functional Affordance Decoherence Theorem

In classical microeconomics, utility is static. In quantum value physics, prior to a transaction, an economic necessity exists in a coherent superposition of functional affordances:

$$|\text{Commodity}\rangle = c_1 |\text{Biological Nutrition}\rangle + c_2 |\text{Social / Cultural Comfort}\rangle + c_3 |\text{Productive Metabolic Fuel}\rangle + c_4 |\text{Emergency Storage Reserve}\rangle$$

We establish two fundamental econophysical theorems:

Theorem 1: The Coercive Wavefunction Collapse of Poverty

As an observer's income approaches the statutory subsistence floor ($Y \to C_{\text{subsistence}}$), budget velocity approaches unity ($v_{\text{rel}} \to 1$), driving the Lorentz coercion factor toward singularity ($\gamma \to \infty$).

Under extreme relativistic coercion, the density matrix $\hat{\rho}_{\text{item}}$ undergoes rapid non-unitary collapse: all higher-order cultural, productive, and buffer affordances are annihilated ($c_2, c_3, c_4 \to 0$). The item is projected entirely into a single brutal eigenstate: immediate biological survival triage. The buyer has no freedom to delay purchase, negotiate price, or allocate funds to education or health.

Theorem 2: Institutional Rent Extraction as Environmental Decoherence

In non-extractive mutual credit systems ($R_a \to 0.02$), transaction channels maintain phase coherence: human metabolic difficulty directly balances physical replenishment without interest accumulation.

When commercial banking cartels and monopoly landlords intervene (e.g., credit cards @ 20.99% p.a., commercial retail leases, and duopoly grocery margins), they couple the physical supply chain to an uncoordinated, high-entropy financial debt reservoir. This coupling induces continuous phase decoherence: the multi-dimensional biophysical and social value of goods is stripped away, forcing all economic interactions to collapse into the single, unidimensional channel of compound nominal debt service.

5. Conclusion & Forward Research Agenda

This treatise demonstrates that the "price of things" is neither an arbitrary subjective preference nor a static scalar number. A physical good or service is a multi-stage Leontief process state entangled with the observer's wealth coordinate.

By modeling economic goods through Relational Value Tensors and Quantum UPE equations, Value Physics provides a rigorous physical foundation for understanding why inflation and rent extraction are experienced as violent, existential compressions by low-income citizens, while remaining mathematically and psychologically imperceptible to high-wealth capital owners.
