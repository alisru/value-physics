We derive the relativistic Lorentz Coercion Factor $\gamma(v_{\text{rel}})$, model the Access-Denial Duality ($A^+ / A^-$), prove the directional value gradient theorem ($\mathbf{V}^7 = -\nabla \mathbf{P}^7$), and demonstrate the systemic collapse mechanics of artificial price suppression ("The Cover").
