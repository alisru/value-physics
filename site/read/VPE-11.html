Executive Abstract

Modern regional communities face systemic extraction under neoclassical financialization: selling raw physical commodities at severely discounted farmgate prices ($DQ \approx 0.13$) while importing processed sustenance, energy, and debt at inflated monopolistic rates ($DQ \ge 4.7$). This structural mismatch drains regional liquidity, induces mortgage distress, and degrades natural substrates ($R_n$).

This research treatise formalizes the Cohesive Village Micro-Economy—a meso-scale computational architecture of 600 individuals across 7 functional guilds. By implementing the Tri-Fold Ledger System (Book 1 Private Mutual Credit, Book 2 Public Sovereign Ledger, and Book 3 Reality Ledger), the WEST Token Difficulty Metric, and Statutory Debt Bifurcation, the village achieves complete thermodynamic and financial autonomy, maintaining a 100% Viability Index ($\Phi = 1.000$) through compound energy shocks and multi-year climate droughts.

3. Structural Analysis: Why the Cohesive Village Succeeds

3.1 Elimination of the "Welfare-to-Corporate Extraction Circuit"

As documented in The Welfare-to-Corporate Subsidy Extraction Matrix, conventional regional towns bleed their purchasing power into supermarket duopolies, utility monopolies, and mortgage banks. In the Cohesive Village, essential sustenance (bread, milk, vegetables, housing, and solar power) is produced and cleared locally using Book 2 sovereign credits. External fiat is reserved exclusively for non-local capital equipment (e.g., medical diagnostics, precision electronics), resulting in a massive net positive external trade balance ($+\$2.94\text{M AUD}$).

3.2 Real Surplus Reinvestment ($I_a - D_a$)

Under The Cover, extractive economies borrow nominal claims to simulate prosperity while real physical surplus is squeezed to zero. In the Cohesive Village:

  • Physical surplus ($I_a - D_a$) is clearly visible in the Reality Ledger as surplus grain (2,523 tonnes) and stored microgrid kilowatt-hours.
  • This surplus directly funds the unconditional support of 150 dependents (elders, children, disabled citizens), community cultural festivals (Plane 4 Lyrical Refreshment), and continuous infrastructure maintenance (Plane 6 Historical Lineage).

3.3 Complete Neutralization of Macroeconomic Interest Rate Hikes

Because the village implements Statutory Debt Bifurcation (Theorem 5), the RBA's 350 bps rate hike had zero inflationary impact on internal village sustenance. Housing and equipment debt amortized strictly linearly at $2,884.62/week, insulating families from mortgage stress and eliminating bank foreclosures entirely.
