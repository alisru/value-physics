protocols, and autonomous development methodology that govern the Value
Physics research repository. Value Physics aims to construct a complete,
biophysically grounded replacement macroeconomic model unifying

deterministic cross-agent handoffs; (4) the historical evolution of the

document sprawl, including the single canonical spreadsheet ledger, the

keep this treatise continuously synchronized with active empirical
sprints.

## 1.1 The Institutional Mission of Alethekanon Research Institute (Division 2)

The Alethekanon Research Institute was established to advance scientific

Traditional economic systems measure value through market prices formed

The primary objective of Division 2 is to deliver an academically

## 1.2 The Trilemma of Autonomous Multi-Agent Research

Executing a scientific undertaking of this scope using autonomous Large
Language Model (LLM) agents presents a fundamental organizational
trilemma:

- **The Sprawl Trap:** Without rigorous structural constraints,
  generative agents produce an uncoordinated proliferation of
  overlapping drafts, peripheral scripts, redundant summaries, and
  orphaned files, leading to context dilution and fragmented knowledge.

- **The Hallucination & Drift Trap:** Left un-audited, language models
  tend to invent synthetic metaphors, import inappropriate disciplinary
  jargon (e.g., borrowing quantum field theory or algebraic topology
  terms without formal mathematical rigor), and substitute qualitative
  rhetoric for verifiable numerical calculations.

- **The Stateless Execution Trap:** Operating within discrete inference
  turns and bounded context windows requires a robust state persistence
  architecture. Without deterministic logging and registry structures,
  agents lose historical continuity upon context compaction, risking
  circular revisions or silent regressions.

To overcome this trilemma, the Value Physics research unit pioneered an
institutional architecture combining strict four-role cognitive
specialization, asynchronous scheduled execution triggers, a single
canonical ledger, and zero-tolerance epistemic auditing.

## 4.1 Phase 1: Foundational Ontology & UPE Formulation (VPE-01 to VPE-12)

The foundational phase established the core mathematical axioms of Value
Physics. The central theoretical breakthrough is the Universal Price
Equation (UPE):

$$P(x,t) = m_1 m_2 \gamma(v_{\text{rel}}) \left[ \frac{S \cdot
U}{R_n (1 - R_a)} \right] + P_e + P_b$$

Where:

- $P_e$: Direct embodied thermodynamic exergy consumed during
  production ($/unit).

- $P_b$: Raw physical material base and resource inputs ($/unit).

- $U$: Metabolic human labor difficulty hours required per unit
  output.

- $S$: Local spatial and seasonal scarcity multiplier ($S \ge 1.0$).

- $R_n$: Resource renewal and biophysical carrying capacity ($0 <
  R_n \le 1.0$).

- $R_a$: Institutional extractive drag fraction representing
  financialized overhead, monopoly rents, and usury ($0 \le R_a <
  1.0$).

- $\gamma(v_{\text{rel}}) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{1 - v_{\text{rel}}^2}}$:
  The relativistic coercion dilation factor, where $v_{\text{rel}} \in
  [0, 1)$ measures the life-or-death urgency and survival dependence
  of the consumer upon the transaction.

- $m_1, m_2$: Structural and organizational markup coefficients.

Crucially, the equation is regularized by the **Cost of Being boundary
floor** ($1/\infty_U = \epsilon_U$), ensuring that $v_{\text{rel}}
< 1.0$ and preventing unphysical singularities during acute survival
crises.
